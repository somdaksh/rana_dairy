What we do and what we do not do

No water added

The milk is not thinned or stretched. What is milked is what is delivered.

No preservatives

Nothing is added to make the milk last longer. It is meant to be used fresh.

Milked fresh daily

Milking happens every morning and the milk goes out the same day. Nothing is stored.

Farm to home

We keep our own cows and buffaloes. There is no middleman milk and no mixing between batches.

Two milks, kept separate

We do not blend cow milk and buffalo milk, so you always know which one you are getting.

Cow milk (Gau Doodh)

Light and mild, and easy to digest. A common choice for children, elders and daily tea.

Buffalo milk (Bhains ka Doodh)

Rich and creamy, with a higher fat content. The usual choice for ghee, paneer, khoya and thick chai.

From the shed to your home

The same simple routine, every day of the week.

  1. Milked every morning

    Our own cows and buffaloes are milked fresh, early each day.

  2. Kept separate

    Cow milk and buffalo milk are never blended, and batches are never mixed.

  3. Handed over the same day

    Nothing is stored. The milk goes out the day it was milked.

  4. Ready for your kitchen

    Call or message us to ask about delivery to your area. Boil before use, as with any fresh milk.

Common questions

How do I order milk?

Call or message us on WhatsApp. Tell us whether you want cow milk or buffalo milk, how much you need and where it should be delivered.

Do you deliver to my area?

It depends on where you live. Message us your location and we will tell you whether we can deliver there.

What is the price?

Prices can change, so we share the current price when you call or message us.

Is anything added to the milk?

No. There is no water and no preservative in it. Cow and buffalo milk are not blended, and no milk from other sources is mixed in.

Can I visit the farm?

Yes. The farm is open every day from 5:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Call us first so we can arrange your visit.

Order milk or visit the farm

Call or message us on WhatsApp to ask about daily delivery, quantities and current prices. You can also visit the farm during opening hours.
