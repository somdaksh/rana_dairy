Privacy policy

How Rana Dairy Farm handles personal information.

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Who we are

Rana Dairy Farm is a dairy farm at Village Madhkarimpur, Khatauli 251201, Muzaffarnagar, Uttar Pradesh, India. You can reach us on +91 89232 85200 or +91 89234 15200.

What this website collects

This website has no forms, no accounts and no comments. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers, and we do not set cookies. The fonts used on the site are served from the same place as the pages themselves, not from a third party.

Like most websites, the server that hosts these pages may keep standard technical logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. These logs are used to keep the site running and secure.

When you call or message us

If you call or message us to order milk, we receive your phone number and whatever you choose to tell us, such as your name, delivery address and the quantity you want. We use this information to take and deliver your order and to contact you about it. We do not sell it or share it with anyone for marketing.

WhatsApp is operated by Meta, and phone calls pass through your telephone carrier. Their handling of your information is covered by their own policies.

Links to other websites

Some pages link to other services, such as WhatsApp and Google Maps. When you follow one of those links you leave this website, and the other service's privacy policy applies.

How long we keep information

We keep order contact details for as long as they are needed to serve you and to keep our own records.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to correct or delete the details we hold about you by calling us. We will act on your request in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them through it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.

Contact

For any question about this policy, call +91 89232 85200 or +91 89234 15200, or write to us at Village Madhkarimpur, Khatauli 251201, Muzaffarnagar, Uttar Pradesh.
