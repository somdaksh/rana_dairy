Our milk

We sell two kinds of milk, cow and buffalo. Each is kept true to itself and never blended with the other.

Cow milk (Gau Doodh)

Light, mild and easy to digest. It is the everyday choice for children, elders and anyone easing into fresh milk.

  • Naturally lower in fat and gentle on the stomach.
  • Good for daily drinking and for tea.
  • From cows raised and fed on our own farm.
  • Daily drinking
  • Tea
  • Children
  • Elders

Buffalo milk (Bhains ka Doodh)

Rich, creamy and full bodied. It is the traditional choice for ghee, paneer, khoya and thick, strong chai.

  • Higher fat content, with a richer taste and more cream.
  • Well suited to ghee, sweets and paneer made at home.
  • From well fed buffaloes, milked twice daily.
  • Ghee
  • Paneer
  • Khoya
  • Sweets
  • Strong chai

Looking after fresh milk

Fresh milk has no preservatives, so a little care at home goes a long way.

Keep it cold

Put the milk in the fridge as soon as it reaches you.

Boil before use

Bring it to a boil before drinking, as is usual with fresh milk.

Use it fresh

It is meant to be used soon after it arrives, not stored for days.

Ordering and delivery

Call or message us on WhatsApp to ask about daily delivery to your area, quantities and current prices. Milk is perishable, so keep it cold and boil it before use, as is usual with fresh milk.
