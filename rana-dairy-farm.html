Our Story

A family farm, run the honest way

Rana Dairy Farm sits in the village of Madhkarimpur, on the edge of Khatauli in Muzaffarnagar. What began as a small family herd has grown into a farm trusted by households across the area — but the way we work hasn't changed.

Every cow and buffalo on our farm is raised and fed by hand. There's no middleman milk, no mixing between batches, and no shortcuts. What leaves our farm gate in the morning is exactly what was milked that same morning.

If you've grown tired of milk that tastes thin, watered-down, or "off," this is what we built Rana Dairy Farm to fix.

What we milk

Two milks, each kept true to itself

We don't blend our cow and buffalo milk — so you always know exactly what you're getting.

Cow Milk

Gau Doodh

Light, mild and easy to digest — the everyday choice for children, elders and anyone easing into fresh milk.

  • Naturally lower in fat, gentle on the stomach
  • Great for daily drinking milk & tea
  • From cows raised and fed on our own farm

Buffalo Milk

Bhains ka Doodh

Rich, creamy and full-bodied — the traditional choice for ghee, paneer, khoya and thick, strong chai.

  • Higher fat content for richer taste & more cream
  • Ideal for ghee, sweets and paneer at home
  • From well-fed buffaloes milked twice daily
