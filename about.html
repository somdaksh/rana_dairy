A family farm, run the honest way

Rana Dairy Farm is in Madhkarimpur village, on the edge of Khatauli in Muzaffarnagar district, Uttar Pradesh.

How the farm began

It started as a small family herd. Today households across the area buy milk from us, but the way we work has not changed.

Every cow and buffalo on the farm is raised and fed by hand. There is no middleman milk, no mixing between batches and no shortcuts. What leaves the farm gate in the morning is what was milked that same morning.

If you are tired of milk that tastes thin, watered down or off, that is the problem we built this farm to fix.

How we work

  • We keep our own cows and buffaloes and raise them on the farm.
  • The animals are fed by hand.
  • Milking is done fresh every morning.
  • No water and no preservatives are added to the milk.
  • Cow milk and buffalo milk are never blended.
  • The milk is handed over the same day and is not stored.

A day at the farm

Every day follows the same rhythm, so the milk you get is always the same honest milk.

  1. Early morning

    The cows and buffaloes are milked fresh. The farm opens at 5:00 AM.

  2. Through the day

    The animals rest in the shed on rubber mats under ceiling fans, and are fed by hand.

  3. Same-day handover

    Milk is handed over the day it is milked. Nothing is stored for later.

  4. Evening

    The farm stays open until 8:00 PM. Buffaloes are milked twice a day.
